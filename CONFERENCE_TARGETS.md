## Recommended Submission Strategy

### Phase 1 — Target NOW (before NB-3 completes)
> Prepare the paper skeleton, ablation section, and HotPotQA section now. NB-3 data fills the MuSiQue section.

**Primary target: NeurIPS 2026** (May 6 deadline) — best prestige, open, strong fit for ML/memory contribution.  
**Co-target: EMNLP 2026** (May 25 ARR deadline) — NLP venue, multi-hop QA is a core topic, slightly more achievable than NeurIPS.  
**Backup: COLM 2026** (March 31 deadline) — check if still open; best thematic fit of all conferences.

### Phase 2 — If Phase 1 misses
**AAAI 2027** (Aug 1 deadline) — gives time to strengthen results, add NB-3, finalize all baselines.

### Phase 3 — Longer horizon
**ICLR 2027** (~Sep 2026 deadline) — after full paper is mature and results are complete.  
**NAACL 2027** (~Oct 2026 deadline) — strong NLP venue once full suite is done.

## Submission Format Quick Reference

| Format | Used By |
|---|---|
| ACL LaTeX (2-col) | ACL · EMNLP · NAACL · AACL · COLING · CoNLL · EACL |
| NeurIPS LaTeX | NeurIPS 2026 |
| ICML LaTeX | ICML |
| AAAI LaTeX (2-col) | AAAI |
| Springer LNCS | ICDCIT |
| ACM (2-col) | CODS |
| OpenReview + ICLR style | ICLR · COLM |

All NLP venue submissions go through **ACL Rolling Review (ARR)** first, then commit to a specific conference. You write the paper once, submit to ARR, and receive reviews within ~6 weeks. Then you commit to whichever conference fits the timeline.
